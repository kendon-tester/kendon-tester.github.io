Privacy policy

Kendon Mail is used only by its owner, on their own Gmail accounts.

  • It reads, labels and drafts email in the owner's own accounts, on the owner's own computer.
  • It does not sell, share or send any data to anyone else.
  • It does not use Gmail data for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
